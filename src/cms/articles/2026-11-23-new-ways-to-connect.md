To make it easier for everyone to stay connected and share in our love of storytelling, we’re excited to introduce our new Facebook and Instagram accounts, giving members and friends another way to keep up to date with our storytelling community.

If you enjoy the socials, please follow us for monthly news, photographs, member stories and other snippets from our storytelling world.

- - -

**Facebook**:

<https://www.facebook.com/storytellerssa/>

**Instagram**:

<https://www.instagram.com/storytellersaustraliasa>

- - -

For background, we have had some difficulties gaining access to our previous Facebook pages, so the committee made the decision to start fresh with new accounts.

We’d love our members and friends to support us by following, liking and sharing our new pages.

And don’t forget our newsletter website  <https://storytellersaustralia.wordpress.com/>, which will be updated monthly following our storytelling sessions.

Thank you from the Committee and our Pied Piper Editor, Charlie-Helen Robinson
