**Winter Workshops cover skills and techniques pertaining to or aligned with good storytelling.**

**Usually on a Sunday there is a Morning Workshop** followed by the usual monthly afternoon of Story sharing.
Themes are different each year.

***Morning Workshop generally...***

**Time:** 10 am (for 10:30 am start) to 12:30 pm (morning tea provided )

**Cost: $20** non-members; $10 members. We ask that people REGISTER then pay cash (make a bank transfer by phone) at the door, on the day.(Underwritten & subsidised by Storytelling Australia SA. If keen, non members can join on the day – yearly membership is $40, conc. $30.)

**Enquiries & Registration**: email [gfross@internode.on.net](mailto:gfross@internode.on.net) (preferable)

**Can attend either morning or afternoon session or both .. f**ollowing the Workshop **all are welcome to stay** and join members from...

* 1:00 -2:00pm for a shared finger-food **lunch**
* $5 Raffle ticket appreciated. A Produce Trading Table also raises funds (so we are always fully insured $$$)
* 2:00 to 4:00pm further fun and enjoyment with **storytelling** by members as they **c**ontinue the theme:of the day.
